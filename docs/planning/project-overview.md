# Gloria Rossi Realty - Project Overview

## Project Vision

Create a professional, elegant website for Gloria Rossi, a luxury real estate agent based in Viera, Florida. The website will showcase her expertise, highlight available properties, and provide an easy way for potential buyers to connect with her.

### Goals
- Establish a strong online presence for Gloria's real estate business
- Showcase luxury properties in the Viera, Florida area
- Make it easy for potential clients to reach out
- Create a site that reflects the premium nature of her services

---

## Target Audience

**Primary**: Luxury home buyers in the Viera, Florida area
- Families looking to relocate to Brevard County
- Professionals seeking upscale homes near Melbourne/Orlando
- Retirees looking for quality Florida living
- Investors interested in luxury real estate

**Secondary**: Home sellers seeking a luxury-focused agent

## Tech Stack

### Chosen Approach: HTML/CSS/JavaScript

We're starting with web fundamentals to build a solid understanding:

| Technology | Purpose |
|------------|---------|
| **HTML** | Page structure and content |
| **CSS** | Styling, layout, responsive design |
| **JavaScript** | Interactivity (contact forms, image galleries) |

### Future Considerations
Once fundamentals are solid, we may migrate to:
- **Next.js** or **Astro** for better developer experience
- **Tailwind CSS** for utility-first styling
- **A CMS** (like Sanity or Contentful) for easy property updates

## Design Direction

### Aesthetic
- **Elegant & Professional** - Reflects luxury market positioning
- **Clean & Modern** - Lots of white space, quality photography
- **Warm & Approachable** - Gloria should feel accessible, not intimidating

### Color Palette (To Be Finalized)
- Primary: Deep navy or rich green (trust, sophistication)
- Accent: Gold or warm metallics (luxury feel)
- Neutral: Warm whites, soft grays

### Typography
- Headings: Elegant serif font (sophistication)
- Body: Clean sans-serif (readability)
